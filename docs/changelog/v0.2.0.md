# Fission v0.2.0

Fission 0.2.0 is a **control-flow structuring** release, and the version bump
to 0.2 marks that the structurer was rebuilt rather than tuned. Where 0.1.9
optimized the decode/normalize pipeline, 0.2.0 replaces the single rule-based
structuring pass with a portfolio of three strategies -- Ghidra-style rules,
angr/Phoenix-style schema match-and-fold, and DREAM-style reaching conditions
-- run over a **live, shrinking CFG** and selected per function by an explicit
quality comparator. On DecBench's 224-binary / 250-function sample set that
cut explicit `goto` count by **70.3% (NIR)** and **68.7% (HIR)**, taking
Fission past Ghidra's own output on the set of functions all three tools
decompile.

Alongside the structuring work this release lands a large expansion of the
type and signature corpora (Ghidra `.gdt` archives, System Informer's phnt
headers, RetDec's PE ordinal tables, angr's WDK prototypes), FID-based
identification of statically-linked library functions, BSim-inspired fuzzy
function similarity search, non-x86 architecture reachability, and an
execution-differential verification tier in `fission-dir`.

## Control-flow structuring

### The live graph

The root architectural problem was that Fission analyzed a *static* CFG and
recorded structuring decisions in side tables, while Ghidra
(`CollapseStructure`), angr (Phoenix), and DREAM all fold a graph that
**shrinks** as regions are recognized -- so each of them gets to match its
next schema against the already-simplified shape, and Fission never did.

`CollapseGraph` is that substrate: a live CFG where folding a region retires
its nodes and replaces them with one, so the next match sees the reduced
graph. Region discovery, the three drivers, and edge concession all run over
it.

### Three strategies, compared rather than ranked

Each published approach is now implemented against the same substrate:

| approach | reference | module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rules over a live graph | Ghidra `CollapseStructure` | `collapse_structure.rs` |
| schema match-and-fold | angr Phoenix | `collapse_driver.rs` |
| reaching conditions | DREAM / angr `condition_processor` | `reaching_driver.rs` (+ `reaching_emit.rs`) |

They do not pre-empt each other. Every driver *offers* a candidate, and
`structuring_quality::StructuringQuality` decides. It compares at three
tiers -- raw, normalized, and full post-layout output -- because a candidate
that looks worse before cleanup routinely wins after it, and vice versa. Its
axes:

- `gotos` must be strictly fewer (the objective),
- `switches` and `empty_if_shells` are hard vetoes (a candidate may not
  destroy a recovered switch or leave empty `if` shells behind),
- `nesting_depth`, `guard_formula_size`, `max_guard_formula_size`, and
  `statements` are budgets, each scaled by how many jumps the candidate
  actually removed.

The `statements` axis was added late and mattered more than expected: it is
what makes duplication-based rewrites (terminal-tail duplication, span
sinking) safe to enable at all, by pricing the text a structuring costs
against the control flow it buys.

Four internal caps turned out to be **guarding what the comparator now
guards directly** -- `MAX_NESTING_DEPTH` (8 -> 64), `MAX_CONCESSIONS`
(3 -> 128), the guard-node budgets (4 -> 512, 2,008 -> 8,000), and the
cleanup span bound (24 -> 96). Each had been chosen to stop bad output
shipping; once the comparator stopped that on the output itself, they only
blocked good output. Caps that bound *driver runtime* rather than output
quality (`MAX_DECISIONS`, `MAX_GUARD_FORMULA_SIZE`) were kept, because the
comparator cannot see runtime.

### Host isolation

Lowering is not a query -- `lower_block_stmts` mints names as a side effect --
so pricing a candidate could not simply be "lower it and look". Both new
drivers were blocked on this. `StructuringHost` gained two entry points:

- `lower_isolated` -- lower on a fork, commit semantic identities only when
  the candidate is accepted;
- `lower_observed` -- price a candidate and discard everything, bounded by
  the shared work-unit budget.

The first thing isolation caught was a match-fold bug that dropped a
function's `return`: a 9-goto function reached 0 gotos with `return r0` gone
and its signature degraded to `undefined`, and **goto density scored that as
an improvement**. A second class -- folds that silently deleted a cycle --
led to `fold_accounts_for_every_internal_edge`, which requires a fold to
account for every internal edge of the region it collapses. Node coverage
alone cannot catch that.

### New shapes and regions

- `InfLoop` -- a cycle with no way out (Ghidra's `ruleBlockInfLoop`).
- `RingLoop` -- a cycle leaving from more than one place, including exits
  that pass through a vestibule block on the way out. The decisive fix was
  that a ring's follow node need not be a successor of the ring head;
  candidates now extend two steps out.
- Multi-terminal acyclic regions, in the shape of angr's `RegionIdentifier`:
  live terminals are attached to one analysis-only `VirtualExit`, candidates
  are forward slices to a real or virtual postdominator frontier proved by a
  closed-frontier plus sole-entry test, and proper subregions are structured
  before their parent. `VirtualExit` is never emitted as a block or label.
  Design and validation matrix: `docs/proposals/2026-08-14-region-identifier-multi-terminal.md`.

One bug in this area was worth its own note: a single region whose frontier
was the `VirtualExit` used to `break` the entire decomposition loop, so one
unfoldable region discarded every region after it.

### Edge concession

When nothing matches and a cycle remains, the driver may concede one edge --
but only after materializing the equivalent guarded or unconditional goto and
its target label, and only if every live node stays reachable afterward
(a node's last way in is never conceded). Jumps whose edges were removed
before the driver saw them are now written out explicitly rather than lost.

### Cleanup

The post-layout cleanup group now runs to a fixpoint (up to 8 rounds), since
each rewrite exposes work for the others: label-alias elimination, terminal-
tail duplication, span sinking into `if` arms, forward-guard inversion, and
jump-only join relocation. New in this release: a guard may be inverted
against the label its block falls out to, a span label may travel with the
span that defines it (when every reference to it is inside that span), and
the span after an `if` may be sunk into it with a mandatory else-arm copy.

## Execution-differential verification

`fission-dir` gained a tier that evaluates a decompiled body, runs the same
machine code under `fission-emulator`, and compares -- built specifically to
guard structuring, on the reasoning that structuring is semantics-preserving
by definition and therefore checkable by execution.

The first version **caught neither** of two injected sabotages (emitting
`while` where `do`/`while` was correct; negating every `if` condition),
because `eval` had no memory model and bailed on any load -- so the functions
it could ground were close to exactly the functions structuring does not
affect. This release adds the memory model, emulator-side scratch buffers for
pointer parameters (`EmulatorHarness::install_scratch` with a matching
`eval::Memory` seeding), and caller-supplied entry register state. The check
now catches the negate-every-condition sabotage with four divergences.

The ceiling is also recorded honestly in the tree: supplying entry registers
bought *no* net coverage -- undeclared reads fell 61 to 56 while unmodelled
call targets rose 15 to 31. What remains is 72 indirect dispatches and 31
calls into real library code; reaching past that means resolving indirect
targets and running callees, which is being an emulator.

## Type and signature corpora

- **Ghidra `.gdt` mining**: `generic_clib`, `generic_clib_64`,
  `windows_vs12_32/64`, and `mac_osx` had never been run through
  `scripts/gdt_extract_structs.py`. Overlap with the existing 13,211-entry
  corpus turned out to be tiny (313/5,500 for windows_vs12, 1/796 for
  generic_clib, 4/1,971 for mac_osx), so ~8,000 struct layouts were sitting
  unused next to data the project already mined. Rust layouts from
  `rust-common.gdt` landed the same way, and GDT extraction now emits typedef
  aliases rather than tag names alone.
- **phnt NT structs**: System Informer's phnt headers define 1,751 structs,
  1,685 of which (96%) had no entry in the win32 corpus. phnt ships plain C
  with no precomputed offsets, so this adds a deliberately narrow C struct
  layout calculator applying MSVC's standard layout algorithm independently
  for 32- and 64-bit pointer widths.
- **WDK kernel API signatures** pulled from angr's vendored prototype JSON.
- **PE ordinal imports**: imports made by ordinal previously resolved to a
  synthetic `dll.dll:Ordinal_N` that downstream lookup explicitly treats as
  unresolvable. RetDec ships real ordinal -> export-name tables for 1,365
  x86 + 28 ARM DLLs (429K entries); these are now merged into
  `utils/signatures/ordinals/` and served by a process-cached
  `OrdinalDatabase`.

## Function identification

- **Statically-linked library functions via FID**: `utils/signatures/fid/`
  already bundled OpenSSL (1.0.1u / 1.0.2l / 1.1.0f), SDL, Qt5, and libsodium
  databases that nothing in `path_config.rs` ever referenced. DIE's
  `DetectionType::Library` can only say "this binary contains OpenSSL"; FID
  hash matching says *which functions* are OpenSSL's.
- FID databases wired for **non-x86** architectures (ARM/AArch64/MIPS/...),
  plus the libc database, and ELF PLT-stub call targets now resolve to import
  names.
- **Fuzzy similarity search** (BSim-inspired, not byte-compatible): iterative
  structural hashing of a function's p-code graph into a sparse feature
  vector, compared with TF-IDF-weighted cosine-like similarity. Finds
  functions that are structurally similar but not byte-identical -- different
  compiler or optimization level, a renamed duplicate helper, a patched
  variant. No `.bsim` import/export and no LSH query server; what is
  reproduced is the shape of the approach.

## Architecture coverage

- **`--language` override**: an audit found 122 of 186 language IDs have no
  `.opinion` path that could ever auto-select them from an ELF/PE/COFF/Mach-O
  header -- mostly niche and embedded targets (PIC, Z80, 8051, 6502, ARM
  v4/v5, embedded PowerPC e500/QUICC, MIPS N32 variants) that never carry
  that kind of container header. All 125,369 constructor templates across the
  133 real language variants are runtime-ready; these specs were simply
  unreachable. Real Ghidra requires manually picking a language for exactly
  these, and now so can Fission.
- Function-start patterns wired for **18 non-x86 architectures**.
- ARM Thumb/ARM decode-mode heuristic, a dead SLEIGH context-register leak,
  Thumb-only retry with forced low-bit code mode, and a fix for ARM's own
  Thumb-tagged entry point truncating its byte window.

## Performance

- `PreHirStmt`'s recursive body fields are `Rc`-ified across `prehir`,
  structuring, `fission-pcode`, `fission-midend-normalize`, and `fission-dir`.
- The linear-body and SESE result caches hand out ownership instead of
  deep-cloning.
- `scalar_ssa.rs`'s out-of-SSA coalescing rescanned every SSA value on every
  interference check (O(values^2)); each union-find group's merged live cover
  is now cached and updated incrementally on union.
- `TraceDag::push_branches` reuses a scratch buffer instead of allocating per
  step; the SLEIGH userop table is `Arc`-shared instead of cloned per
  instruction.

## Correctness fixes

Selected, beyond the structuring work above: dangling gotos left by HIR
presentation and SCCP folds are now caught; globally-referenced labels
survive constant-`if` folding; switch case/default leading labels survive
when goto-referenced; per-occurrence RSP offsets replace one static value per
function; call-result bindings are registered in one up-front pass; ELF
`PROGRAM_HEADERS`/`SECTION_HEADERS` names and PIE `image_base=0` no longer
pollute constant-pointer canonicalization; REP-prefixed string instructions
no longer truncate decode of the rest of the function; DIE confidence scoring
is fixed and Packer/Protector detections are surfaced; typed PE COFF symbols
are preserved.

## Verification

- **DecBench sample set, per file**: 224/224 binaries and 250/250 functions
  decompile; 621 explicit gotos in both NIR and HIR, down from 2,089 / 1,983;
  **zero per-file regressions** on either layer. Every structuring change in
  this release was gated on per-file comparison rather than aggregate totals
  -- one sweep looked like a 41-goto win only because a binary carrying
  exactly 41 gotos had dropped out of the corpus entirely.
- **Three-way comparison** on the 204 functions Fission, Ghidra, and angr all
  decompile: Fission 597, Ghidra 691, angr 420.
- **Workspace suite**: `cargo nextest run --workspace --no-fail-fast` --
  2,509 passing, with the same 7 pre-existing `fission-emulator` SLEIGH-decode
  failures already documented before this release and unrelated to it.
- **Execution differential** re-confirmed after every structuring commit.

## Documented negative results

Recorded so they are not retried:

- A boolean simplifier over guard atoms (`boolean.rs`, truth-table decision
  procedure) fires 45 times on the corpus and changes no output --
  `materialize_branch_conditions` already makes conditions canonical
  variables. Kept as a capability, not as a win.
- Removing the normalized-goto veto was measured twice; both times the result
  was worse.
- Sweeping `MAX_DECISIONS` / `MAX_ROUNDS_PER_NODE` produced no admissible
  gain.
- Two cleanup passes keyed on `PreHirStmt::Block` were written and reverted:
  that variant occurs **once** in the entire corpus. Both were designed by
  reading printed C and guessing at the AST instead of measuring it.
- AST sibling-level do-while recovery fires zero times -- all 131 backward
  single-reference jumps in the corpus are cross-nesting.
